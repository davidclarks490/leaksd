Frequently Asked Questions

What are common signs of a hidden water leak?

Unexplained increases in water use, persistent dampness, water stains, musty smells or warm spots on a floor may indicate a leak. Other issues can cause similar signs, so an assessment is important.

Will every leak require cutting into a wall or floor?

Not necessarily. The inspection method depends on the suspected leak and property. Ask the technician to explain the approach and any possible disruption.

Do you serve residential and commercial properties?

This website describes both service categories. Confirm actual availability and the service area with the business before publishing.

What should I do if water is actively leaking?

If safe, shut off the relevant water supply and stay away from electrical hazards. For major flooding or immediate danger, contact the appropriate emergency service or utility provider.
